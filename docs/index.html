Common Questions

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes. The bundled recipes, images, favorites, shopping list and cooking tools work on your device without an account. External support and recipe-source links need a network connection.

Are subscriptions or in-app purchases required?

No. All bundled recipes are included with the app. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or purchase restoration steps.

Why is a recipe displayed in Chinese?

The app has a multilingual interface. Twenty curated recipes have translated content; the extended collection falls back to Simplified Chinese when a translation is unavailable.

What should I do if the app is not working?

Close and reopen the app, check available device storage and install the latest App Store update. If the problem continues, email support with the details below. Deleting the app removes its local data, so do not reinstall unless you are comfortable losing favorites and shopping items.

Where are my favorites and shopping items stored?

They are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

How do I report an incorrect recipe or image?

Email the recipe title, the ingredient or step that needs correction, your selected language and a brief explanation. Images can be illustrative; use the written instructions and appropriate food-safety guidance.

Reporting a Technical Issue

  • App version and build, shown in Settings.
  • iPhone/iPad model and iOS/iPadOS version.
  • Selected language, recipe title and the affected screen.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and, if helpful, a screenshot with personal information removed.

Do not send passwords, Apple ID credentials, payment details or other sensitive information. Support cannot access your device or recover local data remotely.
