Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Application provider: Yangshuwei

Cook Chinese, shown as 家常菜 in Chinese locales, is a standalone offline cooking app. This policy describes the app and its support website.

Data We Do Not Collect in the App

The app does not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking identifiers or an app-owned data collection backend. It does not upload your recipe browsing, favorites, shopping list or preferences.

Data Stored on Your Device

Favorites, shopping items, language and unit preferences, onboarding state and the bundled recipe catalog are stored locally to provide app features. Deleting the app removes its app-local data. Apple's device backups may include this data according to your backup settings.

Purchases and Content

There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. All bundled recipes are included without additional payment. Any App Store download charge is handled by Apple. The standalone app does not request recipe content from an app-owned server.

Website and External Links

This support website does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms or intentionally set cookies. Its hosting provider may process connection information such as IP address, requested URL and browser information to serve and secure the website. The provider's logging and retention practices apply; the app does not send local recipe data to this site.

Opening recipe-source or Apple links visits third-party websites, whose privacy policies apply.

Support Correspondence

If you email support, Yangshuwei and the email service provider receive your email address and the information you voluntarily include. It is used to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Avoid sending sensitive information. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Changes and Contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated. For privacy inquiries, contact support@cook-chinese-app.newtree.tech.
